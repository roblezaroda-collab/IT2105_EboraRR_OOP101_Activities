YapSpace

A Java-based digital community application designed to provide users aged 13 and above with a space to express their thoughts, feelings, and experiences.


---

## 📌 Project Information

### Project Title

*YapSpace*

### Project Background

People aged 13 and above experience different concerns involving school, family, friendships, relationships, personal experiences, and emotional well-being. However, some may find it difficult to openly express their thoughts and feelings or find someone who can relate to their experiences.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), approximately 1 in 7 adolescents aged 10–19 experience a mental health condition, highlighting the importance of supportive environments that promote mental health and well-being. In the Philippines, the 2019 Global School-based Student Health Survey included Filipino school-going adolescents aged 13–17 and examined areas such as mental health and protective factors.

In response, *YapSpace* is proposed as a digital community space for users aged 13 and above where they can share their thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

The system allows users to create posts, select categories and feelings, and interact with other users through reactions and comments. Through these features, YapSpace aims to encourage self-expression, connection, and being heard in a simple and accessible online community.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

### General Objective

To develop a *Java-based digital community application* that provides users aged 13 and above with a space to express their thoughts, feelings, and experiences while connecting with others through posts, reactions, and comments.

### Specific Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Develop a registration and login system for users.
2. Allow users to create, edit, and delete their own posts.
3. Provide categories for organizing different concerns and experiences.
4. Allow users to select and express their current feelings.
5. Enable users to interact with posts through reactions and comments.
6. Display posts through a community feed.
7. Provide a *My Posts* section where users can view and manage their posts.
8. Apply Java and Object-Oriented Programming concepts in developing the system.

## ✨ Proposed Features

### 1. User Registration and Login

Allows users aged 13 and above to create an account and log in to YapSpace.

### 2. Create and Manage Posts

Allows users to:

- Create posts
- Edit their own posts
- Delete their own posts
- View their created posts

### 3. Category Selection

Allows users to categorize their posts based on their concerns or experiences.

Available categories include:

- School
- Family
- Friendship
- Relationship
- Mental Health
- Career
- Hobbies
- Personal
- Random Thoughts

### 4. Feeling Selection

Allows users to select and express their current feelings when creating a post.

Available feelings include:

- Happy
- Sad
- Angry
- Stressed
- Excited
- Tired
- Confused
- Neutral

### 5. Community Feed

Displays posts from other users, allowing users to browse and read shared experiences.

### 6. Reactions and Comments

Allows users to interact with posts through reactions and comments.

### 7. View Post

Allows users to view individual posts and their available interactions.

### 8. My Posts

Provides users with a section where they can view and manage the posts they have created.
